# SFO Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-PRICE-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Policy Scope
This policy defines surge pricing requirements for rides originating from SFO.

## Standard Surge
- The standard operating surge multiplier is 1.0x.
- Surge pricing may be activated when demand materially exceeds available driver supply.

## Surge Limits
- The maximum permitted surge multiplier at SFO is 1.5x.
- The surge multiplier must never exceed 1.5x.
- Any proposed multiplier above 1.5x is a policy violation.

## Surge Conditions
Surge may be considered when one or more of the following conditions exist:
- Completion rate falls below the operational threshold.
- Request volume increases significantly relative to available drivers.
- Average passenger ETA increases materially.
- Driver supply is insufficient to meet current demand.

## Approval Requirements
- Increasing surge requires an operational justification.
- Surge increases require human approval according to the action-risk policy.
- Increasing surge to 1.3x or above is classified as a high-risk action.
- A surge increase must be validated against the maximum permitted multiplier before execution.

## Monitoring
After a surge change, operations teams should monitor:
- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Request volume
- Active driver supply
- Driver cancellation rate

## Policy Violation
Any recommendation to increase SFO surge above 1.5x must be blocked by the policy validation layer.
