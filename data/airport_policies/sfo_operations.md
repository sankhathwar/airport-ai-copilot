# SFO Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-OPS-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Policy Scope
This policy defines operational requirements for rideshare activity at SFO.

## Pickup Operations
- Drivers may pick up passengers only from designated rideshare pickup areas.
- Drivers must follow airport signage and traffic-control instructions.
- Drivers must not pick up passengers from unauthorized terminal curb areas.
- Drivers must remain within the designated rideshare staging and pickup process.

## Drop-off Operations
- Drivers may drop off passengers at approved terminal drop-off areas.
- Drivers must not wait at terminal curbs after completing passenger drop-off.
- Drivers should leave the terminal area promptly after completing a drop-off.

## Airport Queue
- Drivers waiting for airport pickup requests must use the designated rideshare staging area.
- Drivers must maintain their position in the airport queue unless permitted to leave.
- Drivers who voluntarily leave the queue lose their current queue position.
- Drivers must not bypass other drivers in the airport queue.

## Operational Restrictions
- Drivers must follow airport traffic and safety instructions.
- Drivers must not block traffic lanes, emergency access areas, or restricted zones.
- Airport staff instructions take precedence over normal staging procedures when required for safety or airport operations.

## Compliance
Violations of airport operating procedures may result in temporary restrictions from airport pickup activity.
