# JFK Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-OPS-001

## Airport
John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

## Pickup Operations
- Drivers must use designated rideshare pickup locations.
- Drivers must follow terminal-specific pickup instructions.
- Drivers must not collect passengers from unauthorized locations.
- Drivers must not obstruct traffic or emergency access areas.

## Drop-off Operations
- Passenger drop-offs must occur at approved terminal areas.
- Drivers must leave the terminal area after completing a drop-off.
- Drivers must not wait at terminal curbs for future requests.

## Queue Rules
- Drivers requesting airport pickups must use the designated staging queue.
- Drivers must maintain their position in the queue.
- Drivers who leave the queue lose their current queue position.
- Queue bypassing is prohibited.

## Airport Restrictions
- Temporary terminal restrictions may modify normal pickup procedures.
- Drivers must comply with airport staff instructions.
- Safety and emergency instructions take precedence over normal operating procedures.

## Compliance
Non-compliance with airport operating rules may result in temporary restrictions on airport pickup activity.
