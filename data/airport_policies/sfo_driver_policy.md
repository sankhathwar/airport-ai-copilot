# SFO Driver Policy

## Policy ID
SFO-DRIVER-001

## Airport
San Francisco International Airport (SFO)

## Queue Participation
- Drivers must enter the designated airport rideshare queue when seeking airport pickup requests.
- Drivers must maintain their queue position.
- Drivers may leave the queue voluntarily, but doing so results in loss of queue position.
- Drivers must not intentionally bypass the queue.

## Driver Incentives
Driver incentives may be considered when airport demand exceeds available driver supply.

An incentive recommendation should consider:
- Number of additional drivers required
- Severity of the supply shortage
- Expected duration of the shortage
- Estimated operational impact

## Driver Cancellations
Driver cancellation rates should be monitored as an operational health indicator.

A sustained increase in driver cancellations should trigger investigation into:
- Queue conditions
- Passenger pickup conditions
- Expected earnings
- Waiting time
- Airport congestion

## Driver Conduct
Drivers must:
- Follow airport traffic rules.
- Follow airport staff instructions.
- Use designated pickup areas.
- Avoid blocking airport traffic.
- Maintain professional conduct during airport operations.
