# JFK Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
JFK-PRICE-001

## Airport
John F. Kennedy International Airport (JFK)

## Standard Pricing
- The standard surge multiplier is 1.0x.
- Surge may be activated when demand materially exceeds available supply.

## Surge Limit
- The maximum permitted surge multiplier at JFK is 1.6x.
- A surge multiplier above 1.6x is not permitted.

## Surge Conditions
Surge may be considered when:
- Completion rate decreases below the operational threshold.
- Passenger ETA increases materially.
- Request volume increases significantly.
- Active driver supply is insufficient.

## Approval Requirements
- Surge increases require documented operational justification.
- High-risk surge increases require human approval.
- The proposed multiplier must be checked against the JFK maximum before execution.

## Monitoring
After changing surge, operations teams should monitor:
- Completion rate
- Average ETA
- Active drivers
- Request volume
- Driver cancellation rate
