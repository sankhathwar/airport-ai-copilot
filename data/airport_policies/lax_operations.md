# LAX Airport Operations Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-OPS-001

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## Pickup Operations
- Drivers must use designated rideshare pickup locations.
- Drivers must follow airport traffic signs and operational instructions.
- Passenger pickups from unauthorized curb locations are not permitted.
- Drivers must not block active traffic lanes while waiting for passengers.

## Drop-off Operations
- Passenger drop-offs must occur in approved terminal areas.
- Drivers should leave the terminal curb after completing a drop-off.
- Drivers must not use passenger drop-off areas as waiting zones.

## Driver Queue
- Drivers seeking airport pickup requests must use the designated staging process.
- Drivers must maintain their position within the applicable queue.
- Leaving the queue results in loss of queue position.

## Airport Restrictions
- Drivers must follow temporary airport restrictions when activated.
- Traffic-control instructions may modify normal pickup procedures.
- Safety-related airport instructions take precedence over normal operating procedures.

## Compliance
Repeated violations of LAX operating procedures may result in restrictions on airport pickup activity.
