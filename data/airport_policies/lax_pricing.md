# LAX Pricing and Surge Policy

## Policy ID
LAX-PRICE-001

## Airport
Los Angeles International Airport (LAX)

## Standard Pricing
- The standard surge multiplier is 1.0x.
- Surge may be activated when demand exceeds available supply.

## Surge Limits
- The maximum permitted surge multiplier at LAX is 1.4x.
- A multiplier above 1.4x is not permitted.

## Surge Conditions
Surge may be considered when:
- Completion rate falls below the operational threshold.
- Request volume increases significantly.
- Driver supply becomes insufficient.
- Passenger ETA increases materially.

## Approval
- Surge increases require operational justification.
- High-risk surge changes require human approval.
- The proposed multiplier must be validated against the LAX maximum before execution.

## Monitoring
Operations teams should monitor completion rate, ETA, driver supply, request volume, and driver cancellation rate following a pricing change.
